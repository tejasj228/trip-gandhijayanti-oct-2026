Before you board

Long‑weekend buses and rooms sell out first. The plan is only as good as the bookings behind it.

Booking timeline from today, 19 Sept

  • By 22 SepPick the destination in the group. Vote, don't debate.
  • By 23 SepBook buses both ways — HRTC / UTC sites, redBus, Zingbus, IntrCity. Thu-night and Sun-night departures for 1–4 Oct go first. Window seats on the left for sunrise heading north.
  • By 24 SepBook the stay for 2 nights (3 if stretching). Message hosts directly for group rates on 4–6 people.
  • By 27 SepPre-book activity slots: Bir tandem flights, Kheerganga/Tosh taxis, Sattal kayaks. Confirm local cab for the last-mile.
  • 30 SepSplit costs on Splitwise. Everyone withdraws ₹5–8k cash. Screenshots of tickets in the group.
  • 1 OctReach Kashmere Gate ISBT 45 min early (Metro: Red, Yellow & Violet lines). Carry the ID you booked with.

Pack October, 1,500–2,900 m

  • Fleece + light down or puffer
  • Rain shell (stray post-monsoon showers)
  • Shoes with grip (Triund, Kheerganga, Naina Peak)
  • Cap, sunglasses, SPF — the sun bites at altitude
  • Headlamp / torch
  • Power bank (10k mAh+)
  • Reusable bottle, 1 L
  • Earplugs + neck pillow for the bus
  • Basic meds: ORS, paracetamol, motion-sickness tabs
  • Cash ₹5–8k; ATMs are thin in the hills
  • Government ID (buses & hotels check)
  • A deck of cards. Non-negotiable.

Group rules so it stays fun

  • Bus night: no loud calls after 11. The Volvo is a shared bedroom.
  • Altitude here is mild (max ~2,900 m at Triund). Drink water, go easy on the first evening; no Diamox needed.
  • One person owns bookings, one owns money, one owns the route. Rotate the aux.
  • Return bus is the hard deadline. Last local jeeps/taxis leave earlier than you think — check in the itinerary.
  • Leave the hills the way we'd like to find them. Carry your wrappers down.

Fares, timings and prices are approximate as of September 2026 and move with demand — treat the boarding passes as a planning guide and verify on HRTC, UTC, redBus or the operator before paying.
